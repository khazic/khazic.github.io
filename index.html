  • NVIDIA-NeMo/Automodel PyTorch-native LLM/VLM training framework
    • Speculative decoding (EAGLE / DFlash / DSpark / Domino / JetSpec / MSD): grew this from a single Llama EAGLE-1 recipe into Automodel's full draft-model training stack: P-EAGLE parallel drafting, DFlash, DeepSeek V4 DSpark, Domino online training, and JetSpec causal parallel drafting, plus a vLLM/SGLang serving bridge and an end-to-end tutorial. Extended it to Kimi K3 EAGLE-3, DFlash, and DSpark drafts, Gemma4 targets, and vision-language models through a multimodal speculative decoding (MSD) core and ViSpec VLM draft training.
    • Scaling & evaluation: added sequence packing across EAGLE-1/2, DFlash, DSpark, Domino, and JetSpec; Ulysses context parallelism for the EAGLE-3 draft; FP8 draft training and LoRA adaptation; distributed offline feature precomputation; on-policy target-logit regeneration; and simulated and real acceptance-length evaluation with multi-dataset benchmark sweeps.
    • Model & MoE support: added training support for DeepSeek V4 Flash (plus Multi-Token Prediction) and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Hy3-preview, and Hy-MT2-30B-A3B; brought Kimi Linear to context parallelism with packed sequences and tensor and sequence parallelism; plus Rollout Routing Replay (R3) for MoE RL training.
    • Distributed & VLM training: TP+PP and Context Parallelism for Gemma4 VLM, TransformerEngine attention injection into HF models, and fixes for NaN loss, activation checkpointing, multi-image token expansion, and mRoPE under packing; added THD packed-sequence support for Qwen3-VL-MoE and base-model mRoPE resolution for packed VLM training.
    • Checkpointing & training infra: deferred distributed async checkpoint consolidation, DCP CPU offload, and a checkpoint staging wait option; per-parameter-group learning rates and parallel pre-tokenization before packing.
    • Knowledge distillation: VLM KD recipe with chunked loss and teacher-model offload.
    • Agent / tool-calling SFT: multi-turn dataset adapter, turn-aware history truncation, reasoning-content masking, tool-call accuracy evaluator, SynthTraces agent SFT, prompt masking through mask_generation_prompt, and an SFT tutorial.
  • NVIDIA-NeMo/Megatron-Bridge bridge between HF checkpoints and Megatron-Core training
    • Model bridges: added a MiniMax M3 language-model bridge and recipes, and fixed router expert_bias mapping across the DeepSeek family and the wrong GELU variant in the Gemma-1 bridge.
    • Training & data correctness: fixed a training_log argument-misbinding bug, stale non-persistent checkpoints surviving most_recent_k=0, an Energon test split silently aliasing validation, a MegatronMIMO padding mask keyed on token value instead of position, and a missing pipeline-parallelism guard in QwenVLInferenceWrapper.
    • Speculative decoding: brought DSpark draft training to Megatron-Core with a design doc and the draft heads and loss.
  • verl-project/verl RLHF training framework
    • Added Gemma4 FSDP SFT training support and an ExceptionDumpManager to preserve DataProto on trainer crash, plus fixes for mbridge optimizer config propagation, MultiTurnSFTDataset serialization, multimodal chat_template sync, and SFT trainer token-count metrics.
  • verl-project/verl-SpeCo speculative-decoding drafter-training overlay for verl
    • Drafter backends: ported Automodel's draft-model training into the verl RL stack as pluggable backends: EAGLE-1 / EAGLE-2, Domino, P-EAGLE parallel drafting, and DFlash2, with DFlash2 co-training against SGLang rollout and standalone offline draft training.
    • Fixes: drafter gradients scaled down by world size, P-EAGLE publishing the wrong head and embedding, and Domino curriculum restarts on resume.
  • vllm-project/speculators speculative-decoding draft training for vLLM
    • Fixed an eager-path draft-token mask that inverted additive mask semantics and added a warning when a cached token-frequency distribution is silently reused.
  • hiyouga/LlamaFactory unified efficient fine-tuning framework for 100+ LLMs & VLMs
    • Multimodal (VLM) training, as collaborator: fixing VLM training hangs under ZeRO-3/FSDP via dummy-image injection, lazy-loading multimodal inputs to cut preprocessing disk usage, Qwen2-VL mRoPE fixes, ViT gradient checkpointing, and VLM utility fixes; that work later moved to EasyR1.
    • Data & examples: second ShareGPT conversation format support, a single-GPU full-parameter batch prediction example, and a TRL PPOv2 implementation reference.
  • chatchat-space/Langchain-Chatchat RAG & agent app framework over local LLMs
    • Built the pluggable model-provider callback/validator architecture and its core provider/model manager, including Xinference integration.
